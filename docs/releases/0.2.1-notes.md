# Zemp Lite 0.2.1 — Three panes on every place: release notes (draft)

_Draft of 22 November 2026, written at step 9. It is finished at "Release 0.2.1", after the
acceptance tests are gone through again (step 10), the reader test has been run and the owner has
tried the alpha at its address (step 11). Until then the sections marked "at the close" are open._

## What it is

The first patch of the alpha, on the same engine, the released Zeff 0.8.0 (commit
`6bfa6c55a5584e4e038fc828fef900a30f0856c3`, tag `v0.8.0`): one layout in place of two, decided by
the owner on 21 November 2026 (open decision #203). Its address is `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev`.

## What shipped

**One layout: three panes on every place.** Every place has a left pane, the place itself in the
middle and a right pane, each opened and closed by its own link in a bar above it, and by its own
×; the edge of a pane is dragged, or moved by the arrow keys; each link is also a command of ⌘K.
Each place remembers which of its panes are open, with the project. The **Margin** and **Panes**
links are gone, and with Panes its Output panel, the editor's tabs and the Objects panel.

**Script.** At the left **Project** (the project's files; a click opens a script) and **Outline**,
both closed at first; at the right **Notes**, open at first: the note beside each statement, which
says what it made. **Outline** lists the sections of the script, a section begun by a comment
`// # Title` and a subsection by `// ## Title`; a click brings a section to the top, the section
of the cursor is marked, and **Run section** (on each entry, and in ⌘K) runs a section's
statements with what they need. **Run** unfolds every output under its statement, each as its
statement ends; a run of part of the script (⌘↵, Run to here, Run from here, Run section) unfolds
what was asked for and leaves the rest as it was. The code's column takes 100 characters, the
longest line Zeff's formatter leaves, beside the notes.

**Data.** At the left **Frames of the run**; at the right **Variables**, closed until asked for or
until a variable is chosen. The pane shows the list of the frame's variables (each shown in the
grid or hidden) or, in its place, one variable: its label and type, its rows and how many have no
value with their share, its distribution, and its statistics by its type: for a number N, mean,
sd, min, the quartiles and median, max and the distinct values; for a variable of few values each
value's count and share; for a date or a time its least and greatest. Every number is a cell of
what the engine prints (`summary`, `quantile`, `tabulate`, `n_distinct`, `min`, `max`), on the
frame as the view shows it, so a condition's statistics are its own. The pane is for looking: a
variable is changed in the script.

**Plots.** At the left **Plots of the run**; at the right **The plot**: its statement, coloured as
code, its line (a link to the script), its kind and the size it is saved at.

**Help**, a place of its own, the fourth of the top bar: the engine's page in the middle, the
page's outline at the left and its related functions at the right, each with what it does. F1 on a
name, **Explain** in what a run said, and a field open a page.

**Mended.** In 0.2 the notes were drawn 300 pixels too far right, outside the page, so that a
reader saw none: they stand beside their statements. An unfolded table no longer keeps the page
from narrowing with the window. A file being saved while the project was first read could leave
the window without hover, completion, help and explained pages until it was reloaded: no longer.
Rows asked under a condition while a run starts are no longer answered with the whole frame.

## Breaking changes

For a user's project, none: the views file is read as before (now version 3; versions 1 and 2 are
read, and what they said of the layouts that are gone is passed over). A project opens in the one
layout, with Project and Outline closed and Notes open, whatever layout it was left in. What is no
longer there:

- the Panes layout, with its Output panel and the editor's tabs: every output is under its
  statement, and a second file is opened from **Project** or ⌘K;
- the Objects panel: what a statement made is in its note, and a frame is looked at in Data;
- the Help panel beside the script, and its switch that followed the cursor: Help is a place;
- in Data, **Copy as Zeff** for a change of a variable's type, contrast or labels: the statement
  is written in the script (`to`, `category`, `label`). **Copy as Zeff** for a sort or a condition
  stays.

The Scratch project's two opening comments say what Run now does; a Scratch project made before
keeps its own text.

## Reference versions

As in 0.2: every output is held to what the released Zeff 0.8.0 command line prints, writes and
says for the same script, byte for byte; no other software is a reference for Zemp. New at this
release: the 24 statements the Variables pane asks of the engine, each with what `zeff run` prints
for it (`tests/scripts/properties.json`).

## What is left out, and why

As in 0.2, with this release's own: the sections of a script are read by the interface from its
comments until the engine's language server reports them (#178, Zeff 0.9.0); a variable's
statistics are asked by running the script again with the statements after it, since the engine of
0.8.0 keeps nothing between two runs (C14); no layout for a window narrower than 1,200 pixels
(#33).

## Known issues

- The issues of 0.2 stand, but the first: the notes are where they belong.
- Visits from Safari may go uncounted: WebKit refuses the counter's report to Cloudflare's Web
  Analytics under its access checks, and the report is Cloudflare's own script, not this
  repository's to mend. Nothing else is affected, and nothing about the visitor is sent either way.
- A plot drawn to fit is at most 1,100 pixels wide: in a wide window with both panes of Plots
  closed it does not fill the place.
- A help page is as wide as the engine prints it (118 characters): with both panes open in a
  narrow window it is read by scrolling sideways, or with a pane closed.
