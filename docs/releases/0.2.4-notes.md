# Zemp Lite 0.2.4 — The front door redrawn, and four changes of Help: release notes

_Released 24 November 2026, on the owner's word ("Release 0.2.4")._

## What it is

The fourth patch of the alpha, on the same engine, the released Zeff 0.8.0 (commit
`6bfa6c55a5584e4e038fc828fef900a30f0856c3`, tag `v0.8.0`): a visitor now meets a page that says
what Zemp Lite is before the app opens, and the screens that lead into a project are redrawn.
Decided by the owner on 24 November 2026 (open decision #220, its ten items). Its address is
`https://lite.zeff-lang.dev`; the app itself now stands at `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev/app/`.

## What shipped

**A landing page**, at the root of the address, laid out as a language's home page: the name
large, one line on what it is, and **Open Zemp Lite** with the version and the engine; then the
bands **Why Zemp Lite?**, **See it work** (the Scratch project's three regressions and the table
the engine prints for them), **Made for**, **In version …** (the counts) and **Learn and take
part**, and a foot. Every number, version and output on it is taken from the engine the release
carries when the page is built, and the build stops where one cannot be had. The page fetches
nothing of the engine: that begins when the app is opened.

**The app at `/app/`.** Projects and everything a browser kept are where they were: they belong
to the address, not to a path. An address kept from before, the root with a project after its
`#`, still opens that project.

**An address for a page of Help.** `/app/#/help` and a page after it (a page of Using Zemp Lite, a
function, a package, a code) opens the Scratch project on the Help place at that page. The
landing page's links into Help use it.

**The loading screen redrawn:** the name large with **Open Zemp Lite**, grey until the engine
runs and opened by Return too; a card, **Starting the engine**, with one bar over its three
steps, each with its mark, its line and its state; the four promises in a deep violet band. The
bar glides: however fast the engine starts, it fills over about a second, each step is marked as
the bar passes it, and the button opens when the bar is full. The first step says how the engine
came: the bytes that travelled, or "already in this browser" where the browser kept it.

**The project screen redrawn:** no heading of its own; four cards in two rows, **New project…**,
**Open project…**, **Open folder…** (which waits for the desktop app, and says so) and **Scratch
project**; under them the note on where the work is kept, in two columns; at the right **Recent**,
with its count, five projects in view and the lower edge fading until the end of the list. A
first visit has, in place of the list, a card that opens the Scratch project. On a phone the
screen is one column.

**The name drawn.** Wherever Zemp Lite shows its name on a screen it is drawn as _Zemp_ with a
small raised _Lite_ badge. It is still read aloud, found and copied as "Zemp Lite"; a tab's
title, a report, an export and a file keep the plain words.

**In Help and the script:**

- The hover on a function's name in a script shows that function's usage block alone, the one
  the call would run where a name has two functions. F1 opens the whole page, as before.
- **On this page** is a part of the page: it has no button, no pane and no command. The list of a
  page's sections stands at the right of its text, on the page's own ground, and stays in view;
  in a narrow window it stands once under the page's title. A page with no sections has none.
- A project opens on the Script place, whatever place it was left on. The first click on Help
  then shows its landing page with every section of the Topics pane closed; later in the same
  visit Help returns to the page being read, with Topics as the reader left it.
- Help's landing page is drawn in the look of the new screens: each part a white card with its
  icon in a hexagon. What it holds is unchanged.

**Mended.**

- In Help's search, Return with no match marked opened the page of what was typed even where
  that was no name: for `lm` it showed the engine's sentence that it has no such function, though
  the list showed `reg`. It now opens what was typed where that is a name, and otherwise the
  first match. And a name the engine suggests in that sentence is now a link to its page.
- One rule of a list page of Help named a font the app does not ship. Every rule of the app is
  now read by a test for the fonts it names.
- The loading screen gave the engine's size as 15.1 MB, its size unpacked. See "Corrected"
  below for the size it gives now.

**Corrected: the size of the download.** The notes of 0.2 to 0.2.3 gave the engine as "2.92 MB
with brotli as it travels", and the drawings of this release's screens said 2.9 MB. That figure
is the engine compressed at brotli's highest setting, the least a host could send. The host of
the address compresses a file as it sends it, less tightly: the engine travels as about **4.1 MB**
(4,080,713 bytes for the engine of 0.2.3, measured at the address on 24 November 2026; the known
issues of 0.2 had said 4.07 MB, and the measures did not follow). From this release the landing
page and the loading screen give the size as the host sends it, reckoned at the build, and the
check after each deployment holds that figure to the bytes the address really sends. Against the
limit of risk R4, 5 MB, the engine stands at 4.1 MB. A dated line under the old figure in each
of the four earlier notes says so.

## Breaking changes

None for a project's files. On screen:

- The app's address is `/app/`; the root is the landing page. An address kept from before with a
  project in it goes on to the app by itself.
- **Open a project's zip…** is **Open project…**.
- **On this page** has no button in Help's bar and no command of ⌘K; it is always shown.
- A project opens on the Script place, not on the place it was left on; and what was unfolded in
  the Topics pane is no longer kept from one visit to the next.
- The hover in a script shows a function's usage, not its whole page.

## Reference versions

As in 0.2.3: every output is held to what the released Zeff 0.8.0 command line prints, writes and
says for the same script, byte for byte. New at this release: the table on the landing page is
held, at every build, to what the released `zeff run` printed for the Scratch project's script;
and the usage a hover shows is held to the engine's own usage for each of its 259 functions.

## What is left out, and why

The pages of a function and of a code reordered, and Home and search made aware of the reader's
work (open decision #223): a release of its own after this one, its order of parts still with the
owner. **Open folder…** as a working card: with the desktop app. Sending the engine already
compressed at the highest setting, so that 2.9 MB travels: an engineering experiment with the
host, for a later release. The fonts of a figure (#222): an engine change at Zeff 0.10.0.

## Known issues

- The known issues of 0.2.3 stand, among them: the reader test not run, the checks by hand not
  made, and the occasional wait of two to three seconds before ⌘↵ on GitHub's machines, its
  cause not found.
- A window narrower than 1,200 pixels still says, at its top, that the app is laid out for 1,200
  pixels: true of a project's own screens, though the landing page, the loading screen and the
  project screen now have a phone's layout.
- A test browser does not keep a file of the engine's size between two visits, so "already in
  this browser" is tested by the browser's own record of a kept file, and at the address by what
  the record there says; it is not seen by the routine as a second visit of a real browser.
- Changed in the pages of Using Zemp Lite, and put to the owner to read: in **Finding help**,
  what Return does in the search, the sentences on Home's cards, the Topics pane's sections and
  On this page, and a sentence on the hover; **Open project…** in two pages.
- A function of R that a page's prose names with its brackets is still set as a link (see the
  notes of 0.2.3).

## Measures

Over the twelve deployments of this release, each measured at `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev` from
GitHub's machines in the two engines, unless said otherwise.

- The engine as it travels from the host: **4.1 MB**. Measured at the address at the last
  deployment: 4,081,684 and 4,086,223 bytes in the two engines (from this Mac, 4,081,489 to
  4,085,245); reckoned at the build, at brotli's setting 2: 4,063,624 bytes. The engine unpacked:
  15.1 MB. At brotli's highest setting, which the measure of risk R4 prints: 2,926.6 kB (0.2.3:
  2,925.7 kB). The limit of R4: 5 MB.
- The landing page: 491 kB in 10 or 11 files (its fonts, its styles and a little script), and
  nothing of the engine. To its first drawing: 0.4 and 0.6 s from GitHub's machines; 0.8 to
  2.9 s from this Mac, by the hour.
- The first start of the app, timed to the engine running (the bar reaches its end a moment
  after): 1.1 to 1.7 s in Chromium at all twelve; in WebKit 1.3 to 2.1 s at ten, and 2.4 and
  3.1 s at two; eight of WebKit's twelve within the goal of 2 seconds (0.2.3: 1.0 to 1.7 s and
  1.6 to 2.2 s at most deployments). The redrawn loading screen has not slowed the start.
- A run's figures first drawn: 321 to 570 ms after "done". A plot drawn afresh: 62 to 207 ms.
- ⌘↵ on one statement, from the key to "done": 20 to 69 ms; and in Chromium at four of the
  twelve deployments 2.3 to 3.2 s, all of it before the run began, which is the wait of the known
  issues, its cause still not found.

## The validation suite

The routine on every change, run in full on GitHub in both browsers and on this Mac: format, lint,
types, the edition check, the Rust checks, the native tests of the worker, the worker's message
tests, the measure, the nine sample projects of Zeff 0.8.0 identical to what `zeff run` prints,
the seven tests of the facts the build takes from the engine, 422 unit tests and 635
browser tests in Chromium and WebKit (3 skipped by design); after each deployment, the checks at
the address in the two engines, which now hold the root, `/app/`, an old address, a link into
Help, the landing page's links out of the site, and the size the screens give against the bytes
the address sends.

At the pre-release routine (step 16, on commit `bce5ed5`): the whole routine green on this Mac
and on GitHub in both browsers; that commit deployed, and every check at both addresses passed
from GitHub's machines and from this Mac (one, in WebKit from this Mac, on its second try: the
engine had not come in 30 seconds on a slow line). The thirteen pictures made afresh: seven
came out identical, and six differ only in a time of day or a run's duration shown on them. And
the walk at the address, in the two engines: as a first visitor, the landing page with its
counts and its table, **Open Zemp Lite**, the loading screen to "Ready, 100 %" with "4.1 MB,
once", the project screen with its four cards and the first visit's card, the Scratch project
opened on the Script place, the hover on `reg` showing its usage alone, the run, Help opened to
Home with every section of Topics closed and no button for On this page, the page of `reg`
found by `lm` with its sections beside it, and **Finding help** opened from the landing page
with its picture; as a returning visitor, a project made and then opened by its old address at
the root (in WebKit's test browser, which keeps no project between pages, the old address was
seen to go on to the app).

On GitHub, at this release:

- GitHub's check failed on the commits of steps 6b to 15 and on the two after them, and was
  green on the commits before and on the last. Each of those commits was green on this Mac.
  Two causes, both found only on GitHub's slower machines:
  - The test of the gliding bar, new at step 6b, three times over: it asked for more frames
    than a busy machine draws; then judged the pace between frames; then read its record a frame
    too early. The bar was right each time. The test now notes each change of the screen as it
    is made, and is run at this machine's frames and at a busy machine's.
  - A fault of the project screen, of step 7: until the list of projects had been read from the
    browser's storage, a visitor with projects was shown the card of a first visit ("No project
    yet"), for an instant here and for longer on a busy machine. Mended before any release
    carried it, with a test that finds it on any machine.
- While the check was red nothing was deployed: the address stayed at the commit of step 11
  until the last commit was green. Step 6b and the steps from 12 on were first seen at the
  address at the pre-release routine, which is why the size of the engine was held to the bytes
  the address sends at one deployment only.
- Two deployments (of steps 8 and 9) were reported failed though each was deployed and sound:
  GitHub's own pages answered "too many requests" to the check of the landing page's links. The
  check now asks GitHub's interface for programs.
- The whole-screen test of step 15 found two more faults before any release carried them: with
  enough projects for the list to fade, the fade cut off a row's menu, so its lower items could
  not be pressed with the mouse; and the badge of the name inside the grey button of the loading
  screen stood at 3.9 to 1. Both are mended.
- The number on the loading bar is rounded down, so that it says 100 only when the bar is full
  and the button open; rounded to the nearest, it could say 100 for one frame before.

Not run or not checked at this release: the reader test, carried forward to Zeff 0.9.0 by the
owner's decision at 0.2.1, and the checks by hand (the keyboard alone on a real keyboard,
VoiceOver, Safari itself).

## To try it

Open `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev` in Chrome, Edge or Safari: the landing page. Press **Open Zemp
Lite**, watch the engine start, and choose **Scratch project**. Rest the pointer on `reg` in the
script; press **Help** in the top bar. The address is not announced until the trademarks are
filed (#37).
