# Release 0.2.1 — Three panes on every place

The row of section 15 of the Zemp Specification: "0.2.1 | 0.8.0 | One layout of three panes on every place, each pane opened and closed by its own link (#203): Script with Project, Outline (#178, read from the comments) and Notes, the Objects panel removed and Run unfolding every output; Data with Frames of the run and a read-only Variables pane that shows the list or one variable's properties; Plots with its list and the plot's facts; Help with the page's outline and its related functions; Panes and the Margin link removed; the reader test of Zeff syntax runs on this alpha". The ruling is open decision #203 (the owner, 21 November 2026, confirmed in the Zeff session on 22 November); the mockup he approved is `zemp-lite-three-panes-mockup.html` in the shared notes, second draft. The engine stays the released Zeff 0.8.0: nothing in the worker's engine crates changes.

How to read this file: as the checklists of 0.1 and 0.2. Each step is small enough to finish and commit in one session. **[engine]** marks a hard or foundational piece (the view state and the frame every place stands on, the removal of a layout with the tests that held it, what the worker asks of the engine); **[function]** marks interface work on machinery that already exists. The owner chooses the model for a session from the tag. A change to the normative text is made in the Zeff repository's copy only; a design question the text does not settle becomes an open decision there. A step that turns out too large is split here, not pushed through.

## What the reading found

1. **The copy of the specification** was at Zeff c6d55e7; it is brought to 1beefc7, where #203 and the 0.2.1 row stand, each file taken from the commit and compared with it byte for byte (four files changed: the Zemp Specification, the register, and the catalogue and Language Reference as Zeff 0.9.0's first steps left them, which are plans of the engine's next release and change nothing here).
2. **What stands today.** Two layouts (`views.layout`: the margin editor, `MarginPane`; and Panes, `MainPane` with the left panel's tabs Project and Objects, the editor's tabs and the Output panel). Three places in the top bar (Script, Data, Plots); Help is a panel beside the script (`HelpPanel`), not a place. The data viewer holds its own list of frames and of variables at its left and an inspector at its right; the plot viewer its own list. The view state is version 2 of `.zemp/views.json`.
3. **What the tests hold.** 133 browser tests in 17 files; 14 files press **Panes**, 5 press **Margin**, 3 read Objects, and nearly all of `margin.spec` (32 tests) and `figure.spec` begin from a run that leaves every output folded. Removing a layout and unfolding on Run therefore move many tests; steps 4 and 5 are sized for that, and no test is weakened: a test of the Panes layout is rewritten to hold the same behaviour in the one layout, or removed with the thing it held (the Output panel, the editor's tabs, Objects) and named in `docs/decisions.md`.
4. **The engine at 0.8.0 gives everything the Variables pane needs**, each as a statement run after the script, as the inspector's `summary` is today (the console line's route): `data.summary([v])` (N, missing, mean, sd, min, max), `data.quantile(v, p: [0.25, 0.5, 0.75])` (type 7, R's default), `n_distinct`, and `data.tabulate(v)` (counts and percentages). The bars of the histogram are already asked of the engine for each header. Every number shown is a cell of the engine's print, never formatted by the interface.
5. **The engine's help pages give the Help place its panes**: a page's sections are its headings in capitals (USAGE, DESCRIPTION, ARGUMENTS, …), and its related functions the line under SEE ALSO, each with the first line of its own page.
6. **The outline.** The language server reports sections from Zeff 0.9.0 (#178, step 28 there); at 0.8.0 the interface reads the comments `// # Title` and `// ## Title` itself, by one small reader with unit tests, to be replaced by the engine's when Zemp moves to 0.9.0.
7. **Open decisions due at 0.2.1:** none besides #203, which is decided. Moved here by the owner: the reader test (testing handbook, section 2.4, item 4; one test for Zemp and Zeff 0.9.0, its step 45), the acceptance tests left for the hand, and the check by hand of ⌘↵ in Safari. Risks named by the row: none; R4 is measured at the close as always.
8. **Seen at the close of 0.2, to look at here:** WebKit refuses the visit counter's report under its access checks (step 9); the browser test of resting on a function's name failed once on GitHub (watched).

## Questions only the owner can answer

Each has a recommendation; "go, as recommended" takes them all.

1. **Help beside the script.** In 0.2 Help is a panel at the side of the script that follows the function under the cursor. #203 makes Help a place of its own. *Recommended:* Help is a place only: F1, `:help`, ⌘K and **Explain** on a warning open the Help place at that page, and the panel beside the script goes, with its switch that follows the cursor. (The other course: keep a **Help** link beside **Notes** above the script's right pane, so a page can be read beside the code.)
2. **What a run of one statement unfolds.** #203 says **Run** unfolds every output. *Recommended:* ⌘↵, **Run to here**, **Run from here** and **Run section** unfold the outputs of the statements they ran and leave the others as they were; **Fold all** and **Unfold all** stay.
3. **The statistics of a variable.** *Recommended:* as the mockup, each number the engine's own: for a number N, missing with its share, mean, sd, min, first quartile, median, third quartile, max and the count of distinct values, the quartiles by the engine's default (type 7, as R's `quantile`); for a category, a truth value, or a text or whole number of few values, each value's count and share (`tabulate`); for a date or a time N, missing, min, max and distinct values; for a text of many values N, missing and distinct values.
4. **The example's first lines.** `main.zeff` tells a visitor "Press Run: each statement's note stands beside it; click a note to open its result", which Run unfolding every output makes untrue. *Recommended:* "Press Run: each statement's result opens under it, and a note beside it says what it made." The reader test's sheet is brought to the new layout in the same way; its ten scripts are unchanged.
5. **The reader test and the checks by hand** are yours, at step 11, on the hosted 0.2.1: five to eight readers; the keyboard alone; VoiceOver; a fit's table at the largest code size and in the dark theme; ⌘↵ in Safari. No answer is needed now.
