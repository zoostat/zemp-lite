# Zemp Lite 0.2.3 — The Help place redesigned, its first stage: release notes

_Released 24 November 2026, on the owner's word ("Release 0.2.3")._

## What it is

The third patch of the alpha, on the same engine, the released Zeff 0.8.0 (commit
`6bfa6c55a5584e4e038fc828fef900a30f0856c3`, tag `v0.8.0`): the Help place becomes a place to find
things in, not only a page to read. Decided by the owner on 23 November 2026 (open decision #211,
its first stage), with his changes of 24 November 2026 to its layout and to Home (#211, items 13
and 14), his standing rule that the pages Zemp keeps agree with the engine (#211, item 12), and
his rule for a name with two functions (#215). Its address is `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev`.

## What shipped

**Home.** Help starts on a page of its own: a panel for **Zeff** (its version and its counts, read
from the engine: 259 functions, 10 packages, 144 codes) and one for **Zemp Lite** (its version and
its stage), then, under **Topics**, a panel each for **Packages** (with the ten packages' names as
links), **Errors and warnings** and **Using Zemp Lite**, each with its count and a sentence, a
click anywhere on it opening it. **Home** in the bar returns to it and is lit while one is there.

**Back, Forward and the trail.** The pages opened are a history: **Back** and **Forward** go
through them as a web browser's do, each saying where it goes; ⌘[ and ⌘] (Ctrl on other
keyboards) and the mouse's side buttons do the same while Help is shown, and never leave the app.
Beside them a trail says where the page stands, each step a link: _Packages › stats › t_test_.

**Pages that are lists.** Every step of a trail has a page: **Packages**, the ten with their
counts; a package's page, its functions each with its one line (the shipped datasets under
`datasets`); **Errors and warnings**, the 144 codes in their twelve families; **Using Zemp Lite**,
the app's own pages.

**The Topics pane**, at the left, opened and closed by its link: sections that fold, each with its
count, for the packages, the errors and warnings by family, and Using Zemp Lite. Beside a page it
holds the pages around it (a package's functions, a family's codes), the open one marked and
brought into view, with **‹ All topics** to return.

**On this page**, at the right (it was **Related functions**): the sections of the page open, the
one in view marked, a click going to it. The links to related functions are those of the page's
own _See also_.

**Search as one types.** The field at the right end of the bar (<kbd>/</kbd> puts the cursor in
it) suggests from the second letter, up to eight matches, each saying what it is and why it
matched: a name, or its beginning; a word of a name after the first; a word of a one-line
description; a name from R or Stata (`lm` and `reghdfe` find `reg`, `ttest` finds `t_test`), from
the page's own table where it has one and from the catalogue for every function; a code or its
beginning; and a name one key's slip away. Where nothing is found it says so and offers the
nearest names. Nothing is sent anywhere: the search is over the engine's own index, in the page.

**A name with two functions** (#215). Nine names of Zeff 0.8.0 each name two functions:
`histogram`, `boxplot` and `density` (a layer of a plot, and a plot of a frame's variable in one
call), `plot`, `min`, `max`, `append`, `quantile` and `summary`. Each function has a page of its
own, its title saying what it is called on: _histogram · of a frame_, _histogram · of a plot_. The
bare name opens a short chooser of the two, each with its usage and its one line. Search, Topics
and a package's page list the two apart. F1 on the name in a script opens the one the call would
run.

**Using Zemp Lite.** Thirteen pages on the app itself, in Zemp's own words, each with a picture of
the screen made from the running app: getting started; finding help; projects, scripts and files;
running a script; the notes beside each statement; sections and the outline; the Data place; the
Plots place; commands with ⌘K; keyboard shortcuts; exporting and opening a project; where the work
is kept; reporting a problem. They are pages of Help as any other: in Topics, in search, with a
trail. The owner read the thirteen on 24 November 2026 ("The pages stand as they are"); what
changed after his reading is listed under "Known issues".

**Held to the engine at every build** (#211, item 12). Every piece of code in the thirteen pages
and on Home is held to the engine the app carries, or to the running app: a name is looked for in
the engine's index, what a page says the search finds is searched for, a command is read from ⌘K,
what a page quotes from the screen is made in the app and read there, and each control a page
names in bold is looked for on the screens the page speaks of. A piece held in no way fails the
build. The pictures are made again from the app of each release.

**Commands of ⌘K:** **Help: home**, **Help: search**, and, where there is a page to go to, **Help:
back** and **Help: forward**, each saying where it goes; each pane of Help is shown and hidden by
its command, as **Help: show Topics**.

**Mended.** A fault of the app since 0.2.1, found by GitHub's check at this release: a question to
the language server (formatting, completion, the usage of a call, a hover) asked in the moment
after typing, while the file just saved was still being read, could be answered of the text the
server held before. For **Format the script** the edits of the older text were then made in the
newer one, which garbled it. A question now waits for the text, and the formatter's edits are made
only in the text they were computed for.

## Breaking changes

None for a project's files. On screen: Help opens on Home, not on an empty page; the left pane of
Help is **Topics** (it was **Outline**) and the right **On this page** (it was **Related
functions**), a reader's choice of which is open carried over; the search field has moved to the
right end of the bar; a name with two functions opens a chooser where it showed both pages one
under the other.

## Reference versions

As in 0.2.2: every output is held to what the released Zeff 0.8.0 command line prints, writes and
says for the same script, byte for byte. New at this release: each package's list of functions is
held to what the released `zeff help <package>` prints, and every help page shown is the engine's
print word for word, a page of one function of two being that function's part of the print.

## What is left out, and why

The second stage of #211 (the methods and their index, the pages of the language, kept outputs,
the mark _new_): it needs the engine, recommended at Zeff 0.10.0 and shown by Zemp 0.4. A
one-line description of each package: the engine has none. A project's own functions in the index
and in search (their pages still open by F1 and by the field). Search inside the full text of the
pages. The command line's own chooser for a name with two functions is a step of Zeff 0.10.0;
until then the app cuts the engine's print by function.

## Known issues

- The known issues of 0.2.2 stand, among them: the reader test not run, the checks by hand not
  made, and the occasional wait of two to three seconds before ⌘↵ on GitHub's machine in
  Chromium, its cause not found.
- Changed after the owner's reading of the thirteen pages, and put to him: in **Finding help**,
  the paragraph that says what Home is and the new section "A name with two functions"; in
  **Commands with ⌘K**, the name **Help: home**.
- The words that tell two functions of a name apart are made by one rule from the engine's types:
  "of a frame", "of a plot", "of a result", "of a distribution", "of a list", and "of values" for
  `min` and `max` written before their values. `plot` and `summary` of a fitted model read "of a
  result", the engine's name for the type. The owner kept the nine as they are (24 November
  2026).
- A function of R that a page's prose names with its brackets, as `geom_histogram()` on the page
  of `histogram`, is set as a link like a function of Zeff's, and leads to the engine's sentence
  that it has no function of that name. So since 0.2.1; seen at this release, not mended in it.
- A page that is a list (a package's functions, the codes by family, a chooser) says "This page
  has no sections." in the pane at the right.

## The validation suite

The routine on every change, run in full on GitHub in both browsers and on this Mac: format, lint,
types, the edition check, the Rust checks, 36 native tests of the worker, the worker's message
tests, the measure, the nine sample projects of Zeff 0.8.0 identical to what `zeff run` prints,
377 unit tests and 529 browser tests in Chromium and WebKit (3 skipped by design: what one of the
two browsers' test profiles cannot show); after each deployment, the checks at the address, six
tests in the two engines, one of which opens Help on Home, searches for `lm`, opens `reg`, and
reads a page of the app with its picture. At step 11 the same six were run from this Mac at the
project's own address (`https://zemp-lite.pages.dev`) and passed in both engines, and both
addresses answered as they must (the page, 17 files, the isolation headers).

At the release, on the release's own tree, which differs from the commit before it in the version,
the pictures and the documents alone: the thirteen pictures made again, so that the screens in
them read "0.2.3"; and the whole routine on this Mac, green in both browsers (377 unit tests, 529
browser tests, 3 skipped by design).

At step 11, at `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev`, as a first visitor, in Chromium and in WebKit: Help
opened to Home, with the engine's version and its counts (259 functions, 10 packages, 144 codes);
**Packages**, then `stats` with its 14 functions, then `t_test`; Back twice and Forward twice;
from Topics, the family `W0xxx` unfolded and `W0201` opened; `lm` in the search found `reg`
("matched R lm()") and `ttest` found `t_test` ("matched Stata ttest"), each opened from the
keyboard; `histogram` and ↵ opened the chooser of its two functions, and _histogram · of a frame_
its own page with one title; **Using Zemp Lite**, its thirteen pages, and **Getting started** with
its picture; and Home in the dark theme. The screens were looked at by eye in pictures taken at
the address in both engines.

On GitHub two commits of this release failed their check, and two deployments were reported
failed, each recorded in `docs/decisions.md` with what was found:

- Steps 4 to 6 (`d4a1642`, `b2ff799`): the check of the host after a deployment, not the app:
  files asked for in the seconds after an upload were answered with the page. The check now looks
  again for a minute.
- Step 9c (`0a1ffa3`): a test's own slip: it measured the bar of Help before the line a narrow
  window shows at its top had appeared. The test waits for the line; what it asserts is unchanged.
- Step 10 (`7af0cff`): a real fault of the app, since 0.2.1, found by a test of 0.2.1 in WebKit:
  a question to the language server could overtake the script's text. Mended (see "Mended"); the
  check of the same commit, run again, passed, the fault needing a slow read of the file.

Not run or not checked at this release: the reader test, carried forward to Zeff 0.9.0 by the
owner's decision at 0.2.1, and the checks by hand (the keyboard alone on a real keyboard,
VoiceOver, Safari itself). The keyboard is held by tests in both engines; a person has not yet
walked it.

## To try it

Open `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev` in Chrome, Edge or Safari, press **Open Zemp Lite**, choose
**Scratch project**, and press **Help** in the top bar; type `lm` in the search field, or
`histogram` and <kbd>↵</kbd>. The address is not announced until the trademarks are filed (#37).
