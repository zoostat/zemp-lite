# Release 0.2.3 — The Help place redesigned, its first stage

The row of section 15 of the Zemp Specification: "0.2.3 | 0.8.0 | The Help place redesigned, its first stage (#211): **Home**, **Back** and **Forward**, with a trail saying where a page stands; Home an **Index of functions** by package until the methods arrive with the engine; the **Topics** pane, opened and closed by its link, with sections that fold for the packages (each opening the list of its functions, the shipped datasets under `datasets`), the errors and warnings by family, and **Using Zemp Lite** (thirteen pages on the app, in Zemp's own words); the search field of the Help place's bar suggesting from the second letter typed: names, one-line descriptions, packages, codes, and R and Stata names where the pages give them". Section 7, the row "Help", gains the same in a clause.

How to read this file: as the checklists of 0.1 to 0.2.2. Each step is small enough to finish and commit in one session. **[engine]** marks a hard or foundational piece (what the worker gives, the history of the place, the ranking of search, the frame of the app's own pages); **[function]** marks a part of the screen, or words, over machinery that exists. The owner chooses the model for a session from the tag. Written on 23 November 2026, at "Start release 0.2.3"; no code is written before the owner's "go".

## What the reading found

1. **The previous release is tagged and the tree is clean:** `v0.2.2` on 4ce4342; three commits after it record the ruling on Paper (#172) and mend a fault of the Project pane found after the release, which the notes of 0.2.2 carry. `docs/spec/` is brought to Zeff 635f19f, where the row of 0.2.3 and the register's #211 stand (`docs/decisions.md`, 23 November 2026).
2. **The register.** #211 was decided on four drafts of a mockup kept outside the repositories (`~/dev/handbook/zemp-lite-help-redesign-mockup.html`) and its first stage placed here. What stage 1 holds and what waits for stage 2 (the sections **Methods** and **The language**, the method and language pages with their kept outputs, the green *new*, Home as the **Index of methods**) is in its paragraph "Placed". The drawing is followed for everything stage 1 holds: Home lit while one is on it; **Topics** with its link and its ×, its sections folding with a count; on a page, **‹ All topics**, the page's neighbours with the open one marked and the page's sections under them; search with up to eight matches, each saying what it is and why it matched, a slip of one key forgiven from the fourth letter, and a sentence with the nearest names where nothing is found.
3. **What stands today** (0.2.1, `src/app/HelpPlace.svelte`). The middle shows one page, a function's (`zeff help`) or a code's (`zeff explain`), set in type by `help/read.ts` and `help/HelpPage.svelte`, every word the engine's; the left pane **Outline** holds the page's sections; the right pane **Related functions**; the bar a field that opens a page by its exact name. There is no page to start from, no way back, no search. The workspace keeps one page (`workspace.help`), not a history.
4. **What the engine of 0.8.0 gives, and what it does not.** The worker builds the released engine in (Zeff `v0.8.0`, 6bfa6c5), so it can read the engine's registry through its public interface, as it reads a help page now: the packages shipped, in the tiers' order (`packages_shipped`); each function's name and one-line summary; the datasets; and each catalogue row's `equivalent` ("R t.test(); Stata ttest"). A package's page is `zeff help <package>`'s print, which the Help place already sets; _corrected at step 2:_ for `text`, `plot` and `datasets` that command prints the page of the function of the same name, so a package's page is set from the index of step 2 for every package alike (`docs/decisions.md`, 23 November 2026). The codes by family, each with its kind and title, are given already (`codesExplained`). The engine carries **no one-line description of a package** (the mockup's "Hypothesis tests, analysis of variance and correlation" is stage 2's or a later engine's); none is written here.
5. **R and Stata names.** Of the 250 help pages of 0.8.0, 106 have the section *Coming from R or Stata*; the others are revised by Zeff 0.10.0 (decision 103). The catalogue's `equivalent` gives one for every function. Question 1.
6. **Back and Forward from the keyboard and the mouse.** In the browsers ⌘[ and ⌘] and the mouse's side buttons are the browser's own Back and Forward, which would leave the app. Whether they are taken by the Help place's history (entries of the browser's own history while Help is shown) or left to the browser and given other keys is an engineering choice, settled and recorded at step 3, with the rule that nothing the reader does in Help takes them out of the app.
7. **The pages of Using Zemp Lite** are the one exception to the rule that every word of Help is the engine's (#211, item 9): Zemp's own words, kept in this repository, each with a picture of the screen where a method has its example. A picture taken by hand goes stale with each release, so they are made from the running app by a script, and a page that names a control is checked by a test to name one that is on the screen.
8. **Open decisions due at 0.2.3:** #211, decided. Risks the row touches: R4 (the build grows by the pictures and the index; measured at each step), R10 (a reader who cannot find a function is a reader lost to R or Stata).
9. **Carried from 0.2.2, not part of this row:** the reader test (owed at Zeff 0.9.0) and the checks by hand, the owner's; the copy of the specification brought up again when the Zeff session enters #172.

As in 0.2.2 (`npm run routine`): format, lint and type checks; the edition check; the worker's `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy` with warnings denied and `cargo test`; the worker's messages under Node; the measure of the worker against R4; the nine sample projects of the released engine through the worker, byte for byte; the unit tests; the browser tests in Chromium and WebKit. After each green push the build is deployed and checked at both addresses. A step is ticked when the routine is green here and on GitHub.

## Questions only the owner can answer

Each has a recommendation; "go, as recommended" takes them all.

1. **Where search finds R and Stata names.** The row says "where the pages give them", and 106 of 250 pages do. _Recommended:_ from the page's *Coming from R or Stata* where it has one, and from the catalogue's `equivalent` column for every function, which is the engine's own data; so `lm` finds `reg` and `ttest` finds `t_test` whichever page is revised. A match found in the catalogue says so ("matched R lm()"), as a page's does.
2. **What Home shows of each function.** _Recommended:_ the names alone, in columns under each package's heading, as the mockup draws the methods; the one line is shown when the pointer or the keyboard rests on a name, on the package's page, and in search. The other way: each name with its one line, a long page in one column.
3. **The words of the thirteen pages of Using Zemp Lite.** _Recommended:_ drafted in these steps from the app as it is, short and in plain words, each picture made from the app in the light theme; the thirteen put to the owner to read before the release (step 9b), and changed on his word.
