# Release 0.2.5 — The app back at the root, and the engine sent packed tight

The row of section 15 of the Zemp Specification: "0.2.5 | 0.8.0 | The app back at the root of `lite.zeff-lang.dev` (#225): the landing page of 0.2.4 removed with its tests, an address with `/app/` in it going on to the same place at the root, a page of Help at `/#/help/…`, and the loading screen's button named **Start**; then, as its second step, the engine sent packed tight at Brotli's highest setting (#224), about 2.9 MB in place of 4.1 MB, tried at the address first and left out if the host cannot send it so". (The button's name was added to the row on 24 November 2026, after the plan and the owner's "go", on his word of that day: #225, item 5; step 4b below.)

How to read this file: as the checklists of 0.1 to 0.2.4. Each step is small enough to finish and commit in one session. **[engine]** marks a hard or foundational piece (the address the app lives at, how the host sends the engine); **[function]** marks a screen, words or a clearing-out over machinery that exists. The owner chooses the model for a session from the tag. Written on 24 November 2026, on the owner's word of that day (open decision #225), before any code.

## What the reading found

1. **The previous release is tagged and the tree is clean:** `v0.2.4` on fc8567f; the commits after it change documents alone (the copy of the specification, the owner's proposal of the tight packing, his opening of this release). `docs/spec/` is brought to Zeff b1597bf, where the row of 0.2.5, #225 with his four answers, and #224 placed as this release's second step stand. Zeff 0.9.0 is not released, so the engine is Zeff 0.8.0, as in 0.2 to 0.2.4.
2. **What 0.2.4 made of the address, and what is undone.** The build makes two pages over one `/assets/`: the landing page at the root (`index.html`, `src/root/`) and the app at `/app/` (`app/index.html`, `src/main.ts`); a root address with a project after its `#` goes on to `/app/` (`src/root/forward.ts`). #225 turns this about: the app at the root, and `/app/` a page that only goes on. What a browser kept (projects, and the engine under its address in `/assets/`) belongs to the origin and to that address, so nothing kept is lost by the move, as nothing was at 0.2.4.
3. **The app's own sources hardly name the address.** No screen of the app writes `/app/`; the address of a project and of a page of Help is what follows the `#`. What names `/app/` is the tests: about fifty files of browser tests open it, the checks at the address (`tests/host/host.spec.ts`, `tools/check-host.mjs`), the pictures' tool and the edition check's test.
4. **What only the landing page used.** Its page and its helpers (`src/root/`: the page, the words, the colours of the printed table, the going-on); its tests (`landing.spec.ts`, its states in `front-whole.spec.ts`, its checks at the address: the links out of the site, its weight and first drawing); of the facts the build takes from the engine (`tools/facts.mjs`), all but one: the loading screen uses the travelling size alone, and the engine's version, the three counts, the Scratch script and its print are shown nowhere else from the build (Help's Home has its counts from the engine as it runs); and the build's stop where a release's notes are not written, which was there because the landing page linked to them.
5. **What stays of 0.2.4** (#225, item 3; #220, items 3 to 10): the loading screen with its line and **Open Zemp Lite**, the gliding bar, the project screen, the name drawn, the four changes of Help, the page **What is counted**, and the address for a page of Help, now `/#/help/…`.
6. **The words.** No page of **Using Zemp Lite** names the landing page or `/app/`; `README.md` does not either. The loading screen's line was drawn as "the landing page's line": it stays, as its own. Step 3 reads every page and picture again for what the move changes, and expects none.
7. **The tight packing (#224), as measured on the engine of 0.2.4:** 15,148,342 bytes unpacked; 4,058,127 as the host sends it now; 2,918,859 at brotli's highest setting. The host, Cloudflare Pages, packs a file as it sends it and does not of itself send one packed beforehand. The way to be tried: the build writes the engine packed beside the engine, and a small function at the host answers the engine's address, and that address alone, with the packed file marked as brotli and untouched; a browser that does not take brotli is given the engine as now. The deployment is made by Wrangler from GitHub (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`), which carries such a function with the built files.
8. **The trial must not break the address.** A fault in how the engine is sent leaves the app unable to start. So the trial is made first on a deployment beside the real one (a preview of the host, under an address of its own), and the checks at the address are run against it; the real address is given the packed engine only when they pass there.
9. **Open decisions due at 0.2.5:** #225, decided; #224, placed here as the second step, falling if the host cannot do it. Risk the row touches: R4 (the download: 4.1 MB of 5 MB today, about 2.9 MB if #224 holds; the rating is the owner's).
10. **Carried from 0.2.4, not part of this row:** the reader test (owed at Zeff 0.9.0) and the checks by hand, the owner's; the pages of Help reordered (#223), a release of its own after this one.
