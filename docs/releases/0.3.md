# Release 0.3 — The engine of Zeff 0.9.0, the examples of Help run in the page, and Paper

The row of section 15 of the Zemp Specification: "0.3 | 0.9.0 | The preview gains the release's methods through the engine (the binary models as `glm` and the ROC curve as `curve`, #112). Its own additions are two. The examples of the Help place run in the page (#211, item 11): a **Run** button under every example, the output, warnings, errors and plots drawn by the app's engine in a session of their own, each example running by itself when its page opens, and **Copy to script**. And **Paper** for `.typ` files, its first stage (#172, option A): the source beside its live page; a click on the page finds the source; an outline; tables and figures from scripts through `export` (`.typ` and SVG), refreshed by **Run**; the PDF set by Typst in the browser, Typst being a module of Zemp's own that is fetched the first time a paper is opened and is never inside the engine or the bundle a page loads at its start; and **Export script with output** as PDF".

How to read this file: as the checklists of 0.1 to 0.2.5. Each step is small enough to finish and commit in one session. **[engine]** marks a hard or foundational piece (the engine the app carries, a session of the worker, the Typst module, how the host sends a file, the setting of a page); **[function]** marks a screen, words or a clearing-out over machinery that exists. The owner chooses the model for a session from the tag. Written on 27 November 2026, on the owner's word of that day ("ready to start 0.3"), before any code.

## What the reading found

1. **The previous release is tagged and the tree is clean:** `v0.2.5` on b6ded13; the three commits after it change a test's helper and documents. **Zeff 0.9.0 is released:** the tag `v0.9.0` on commit 670e73f of the Zeff repository (26 November 2026), a GitHub release with its notes. `docs/spec/` is brought to Zeff f0c83be, compared byte for byte; two files differ from the copy at 6af2614, the register of open decisions (#226 to #230, and the status at the close of 0.9.0) and the risk register (the review at 0.9.0).
2. **What the engine of 0.9.0 brings to the app with no work of Zemp's but carrying it:** the package `glm` (`glm`, `curve`, `confusion`), `margins`, `summary(exponentiate: true)`, `timer`, `n_chars`, `row_number`, `row(vars, stat: n_missing)`, `posthoc(method: games_howell)`; a tenth sample project (`samples/binary`); 84 help pages in the fuller form (a caption on every example, missing values under their own heading, a common mistake with its real message, the equivalents in R and Stata). The worker pins the engine by the commit of `v0.8.0` in nine places (`worker/Cargo.toml`).
3. **What 0.9.0 changes that a carried script or page may meet** (its notes, "Breaking changes"): a warning repeated in a loop printed once with its count; six printed layouts; an imported column of whole numbers widened to Float at a decimal; the note under a weighted test; `compare` refusing fits that do not belong together; a script's own function no longer called by a name the release adds. The Scratch project, the code of the thirteen pages of **Using Zemp Lite** and the pictures are held to the carried engine at every build (#211, item 12), so the build says where any of these bites.
4. **What the editor asked of the engine, now given** (#175: C5, C6, C22; #197, which is C16; #178): each statement's result with its type, its dimensions and what it depends on, and its print made again at another width; the plots of a run kept by the browser build and laid out at another size and theme without a new run; an error's chain of calls as fields, with the pass of a loop; the sections of a script reported by the language server. The app carries stand-ins of its own for some of these, each recorded as lasting "until the engine": the sections read from the comments (`src/editor/sections.ts`, 0.2.1, step 3), and the plot of a name bound twice not drawn (`boundPlots` in `src/output/run.ts`, a known limit of 0.2).
5. **The examples of Help today.** A page's examples are read from the engine's page as lines typed (`> `) and lines printed (`src/help/read.ts`), and shown as kept text. Nothing runs them. The worker has one session, the reader's project.
6. **Paper today.** Nothing of it exists: no file of the app names Typst or `.typ`; the app opens `.zeff`, `.toml`, `.txt` and `.md` in a tab and nothing else. `export` writes `.typ` and SVG since Zeff 0.8.0, and the worker gives back the files a run wrote (`worker/src/written.rs`). Measured at Zeff 0.8.0 (steps 21b and 21c): Typst sets the same bytes on every target; about 26 MB, 7.2 MB compressed; 14 of its 48 sets of hyphenation patterns are under licences this project does not link, and cannot be left out as published.
7. **What the record of 23 November 2026 asks of this plan:** the spike of the Typst module before anything is built on it, to settle four things: the licences of everything linked and shipped, the fonts among them (the register asks for New Computer Modern, the Article template's default at stage 2, to be checked; the font Typst sets mathematics in by default is of that family); the size as it travels; the time to set a page in the browser; and the PDF's bytes against the desktop Typst's for the same document. The plan adds a fifth, since two pieces of the row rest on it: whether a place on the set page can be traced back to its place in the source.
8. **The project's folders** (Engine Specification, section 2) name `scripts/`, `functions/`, `data/` and `output/`, and no place for a paper. Where a new paper is made is a default, and the owner's (question 3).
9. **Open decisions due at the plan of 0.3:** #209, what the Variables pane shows for each kind of variable (open; A recommended there; question 1). **Decided and placed here by its own words:** #228, a project's other text files opened to be read, "the release of #223 if it comes before 0.3, otherwise 0.3"; #223's release has not come, so it is in this plan, the owner's to move. **Decided, in the row:** #112, #172 (option A), #211 item 11 (an example runs by itself when its page opens).
10. **Risks the row touches.** R4 (the download): the engine travels as 2.93 MB of 5 MB at 0.2.5, and grows with a new package; measured at step 3. The Typst module is outside that measure by the row's own words, and a test holds that the start of the app fetches nothing of it. R13 (a browser keeping a wrong file for a year): its remedy for the app's other built files was promised for this release, and the Typst module is one more built file to guard (step 4). R10 (adoption): the preview gains its first new methods since it opened.
11. **Carried, not part of this row:** the reader test, moved by the owner to Zeff 0.11.0 (#230), on a Zemp Lite that carries a newer engine, which this release is; the checks by hand, the owner's; one unexplained failure of a browser test (**Run to here**, WebKit, 24 November 2026), to be looked into at a second sighting.

## The routine of this repository

As in 0.2.5 (`npm run routine`): format, lint and type checks; the edition check; the worker's `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy` with warnings denied and `cargo test`; the worker's messages under Node; the measure of the worker against R4; the sample projects of the released engine through the worker, byte for byte (ten from step 3); the tests of what the build takes from the engine and of the host's function; the unit tests; the browser tests in Chromium and WebKit. From step 6 the Typst module has the same checks of its own (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy` with warnings denied, `cargo test`, its measure). After each green push the build is sent to the trial address, checked there, and then to the address, and checked again in the two engines. A step is ticked when the routine is green here; GitHub's check of its commit is read before the next step is begun.

## Questions only the owner can answer

1. **The Variables pane for each kind of variable (#209, due at this plan).** A (recommended in the register): every kind in its table, here (long text by the lengths of its values with five values in full; text of few values with a line naming `category()`; a variable whose every value differs said to look like an identifier; an ordered category in its levels' order; an opaque column counted with its first values); two steps, 20 and 21. B: long text alone; step 20 alone. C: nothing at 0.3.
2. **What Run does in the Paper place.** A (recommended): it runs the scripts whose exported files the paper uses, in the order of their names, and the page is set again with what they wrote; a paper that uses none says so. B: it runs the script that was last open in Script, and nothing else.
3. **Where a new paper is made.** Recommended: **New paper…** asks its name and makes it in a folder `paper/` of the project (`paper/paper.typ` for the first), and a `.typ` file anywhere in a project opens in Paper. The project's folders in the Engine Specification name no place for a paper, so the answer goes to the coordinating session for the register.
4. **Whether the Scratch project carries a short sample paper.** Recommended: yes, one page that sets the table and a figure its scripts export, so that a first visitor meets Paper with something on the page; it is fetched and set only when Paper is opened.

To be shown to him as they are met, not asked now: what the spike finds of the licences, the fonts above all, with the fonts a paper may be set in if one cannot ship (step 2); a sample PDF of **Export script with output**, whose printed layout is his (step 18); the pages of **Using Zemp Lite** that are new or changed, to read (step 22).
