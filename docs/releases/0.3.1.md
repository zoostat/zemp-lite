# Release 0.3.1 — Zemp Lite redrawn: the revamp, cobalt, and computers only

The row of section 15 of the Zemp Specification: "0.3.1 | 0.9.0 | Zemp Lite redrawn, with nothing it can do taken away (zeff_open_decisions.md, #245): the front page, the projects screen as a table, one slim top bar and a line below it with the console, a side menu of the places drawn as icons named on hover (#33 amended), a toolbar row in Script and in Paper, the Project panel in Script and Paper, Data's frames as a menu, Plots as a strip keeping every layout, Help as an index, a page and its contents; cobalt as the accent (#244); made for computers, a tablet held sideways counted as one and a page below 1,000 pixels saying so (#243)". It carries the same engine as 0.3, Zeff 0.9.0: a Zemp release never needs an engine release.

Where this plan comes from: a draft of the coordinating session (28 November 2026, on the owner's word "Now plan this visual changes for release 3.1"), approved by the owner there ("Yes, all three") and adopted here on his word "Start release 0.3.1", after the reading the rule for starting a release asks and with what that reading corrected (finding 10).

How to read this file: as the checklists of 0.1 to 0.3. Each step is small enough to finish and commit in one session. **[engine]** marks a foundational piece (the colours every screen draws from, what every test of a screen stands on, the frame every place sits in, the release's routine); **[function]** marks a screen or words over machinery that exists. The owner chooses the model for a session from the tag.

## What the reading found

1. **0.3 is released and the tree is clean.** The tag `v0.3.0` is on 8578fd2; the three commits after it change documents only (the record of the deployment, and two notes of 28 November on the rulings and on this plan). The engine stays Zeff 0.9.0.
2. **The three rulings, in the Zeff register** at its commit 3357e40: #243 (no phones; a tablet held sideways a small laptop, option A), #244 (cobalt), #245 (the revamp, completed on 28 November; #33 amended for the side menu alone), and the row of 0.3.1 in section 15. Eight files of `docs/spec/` here are behind the Zeff repository; step 1 brings them to 3357e40, compared byte for byte. The mockup the owner approved is outside the repositories (`~/dev/handbook/zemp-lite-revamp-mockup.html`, its figures beside it); every number in it was printed by the engine. It is read at each step, at 1,280 × 800 and at 1,024 × 768.
3. **What the code holds today.** The colours live in one file, `src/theme/tokens.ts`, with `tokens.test.ts` beside it; the old accent is also written by hand in `public/counted.html` (the page "What is counted"), and four tokens carry the violet in their name or their use (`violet-2`, `violet-3`, the `deep` band of the loading screen, the `tint` ground). The frame is in `src/app/`: `TopBar.svelte` (which holds the five places as words), `Place.svelte` (the bar of each place, with a link for each pane, #203), `EngineLine.svelte` and `ConsoleLine.svelte` (the engine's line and the console), `Workspace.svelte`, `NarrowLine.svelte` (the note under 1,200 pixels). Which panes are open is kept with a project in `.zemp/views.json` (`src/app/views.ts`, version 5). The places: `src/data/DataViewer.svelte`, `src/plots/PlotViewer.svelte` (Fit, One column, Two columns, Slide, Compare, Pop out), `src/app/PaperPlace.svelte`, `src/app/HelpPlace.svelte` with `src/help/`. The front page is `src/app/Start.svelte` with `FrontBar.svelte`; the projects screen is `src/app/Welcome.svelte`; each has the watermark (`src/theme/Watermark.svelte`).
4. **What exists already of what the revamp draws,** so is moved and not built: the search of Help by an R or a Stata name (0.2.3); a function's card under the pointer, which shows its usage (0.2.4); **Explain** on a warning; "On this page" and "See also"; **Fold all** and **Unfold all**; **Copy as Zeff**; **Keep rows where** and **Search the cells**; every layout of Plots.
5. **What is new capability, not only a new look,** each with tests of its own: **Export all projects…** (one zip for each project, as **Export project…** writes); the space the browser has used, under the storage note (the browser's own estimate, left out where it gives none); the places by ⌘1 to ⌘5; **Try in the console** under an example; a function's card naming what it matches in R and Stata; the state of each project's last run in the table; a selected column's summary in the line under the grid.
6. **The tests that hold today's layout.** 58 files of browser tests, 825 tests in the two engines; 39 files set a window's size; six set a width under 1,000 pixels (375, 390 and 760, and a popped-out plot's own window at 900 and 600, which is not the app's and stays). `using-controls.spec.ts` holds every control a page of **Using Zemp Lite** names to the screen, so a step that moves or renames a control changes that page's words in the same commit. The 18 checks at the address (`tests/host/`) press the deployed build after every push, so they too move with each step.
7. **The pictures of Help.** 14 pictures in `src/using/pictures/` and 14 pages of **Using Zemp Lite**. Every picture changes; #220's rule (Help agrees with the build) holds them to the release.
8. **Open decisions due at this plan:** none is open. #243's tablets were due "at the Zemp Lite release that next changes its layout" and were decided on 28 November (option A).
9. **Risks the row touches.** R4 (the download): the icons and styles add a few kilobytes against the 5 MB ceiling, measured at the last step. R10 (adoption): the first screen a visitor sees is new. R13 (a browser keeping a wrong file): the remedy of 0.3 holds for every built file; nothing is added.
10. **What the reading corrected in the draft, and the engineering it left unsaid.**
    1. The tag is `v0.3.0`, not `v0.3`; there are 14 pages, not 13; 58 files of browser tests, not 60.
    2. **The order.** "Computers only" is built before the frame and the places, not after them: otherwise every redrawn screen would have to pass the tests of a phone's layout that the release then removes. #245 leaves the order of building to engineering.
    3. **"Every screen is tested at 1,024 × 768" (#243)** is held by a whole-screen suite: every place and every state of it, in both themes and both engines, with nothing hidden, cut or overlapping and no sideways scroll. The 825 tests are not each run twice.
    4. **Touch targets of 32 pixels** apply where the pointer is a finger (the browser says so); with a mouse the drawn sizes hold (the line of 26 pixels, the grid's rows of 25).
    5. **The notice below 1,000 pixels** has a way on, since #243 says a visitor "may go on at their own risk": **Go on anyway**, remembered for the visit. A popped-out plot's window is not the app and never shows it.
    6. **⌘1 to ⌘5 are also a browser's own keys for its tabs.** The app takes them while it has the keyboard; each place is reached as well by its icon and by ⌘K. Whether Chrome, Safari and Firefox themselves give the keys up is one of the checks by hand, and is told to the owner if a browser keeps them.
    7. **Under the top bar today** stand the engine's line and each place's own bar. Step 4 and step 5 redraw the top bar, the side menu and the line below; each place's bar stays as it is until that place's own step makes it the row #245 draws.
    8. **The view state** (`.zemp/views.json`) goes to version 6 where a pane becomes a menu or a strip; a file of an earlier version opens with nothing lost.
    9. **A script exported with its output, and a paper,** carry no colour of the interface: their kept digests must not change at step 2, which the tests of 0.3 already hold.
    10. **Section 5 of the Zemp Specification and the design guide** are the Zeff repository's and the handbook's, amended by the coordinating session; this repository records what was built for it (the last two steps) and refreshes its copy.
    11. **Each green push goes to the address,** as in every release: between step 2 and step 13 the address shows an app redrawn in part, marked `0.3.1-dev` and alpha.

## What 0.3.1 leaves out

Everything of Zemp 0.4: Paper's second stage (the settings file, `refs.bib`, the Article template) and the index of methods. The pages of Help reordered (#223), a release of its own. **Open folder…** as a working card (desktop, 0.5). The website's change of colour (#244 says "when it is next rebuilt", which is not a Zemp release) and the editors' colour files, which are the Zeff repository's. No new function of the catalogue belongs to a Zemp release.

## The routine of this repository

As at 0.3 (`npm run routine`): format, lint and type checks; the edition check; the two crates' `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy` with warnings denied and `cargo test`; the worker and the Paper module under Node; the measures against R4; the ten sample projects through the worker, byte for byte; the host's function; the unit tests; the browser tests in Chromium and WebKit. From step 2, a test that none of the old accent's values is left in the built files. From step 3, the whole-screen suite at 1,024 × 768 beside the laptop's size. After each green push the build goes to the trial address, is checked there, then goes to the address and is checked again in the two engines. A step is ticked when the routine is green here, and GitHub's check of its commit is read before the release is closed.
