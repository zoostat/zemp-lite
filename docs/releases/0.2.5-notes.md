# Zemp Lite 0.2.5 — The app back at the root, and the engine sent packed tight: release notes

_A draft, written at step 5 of the release; completed at the pre-release routine and dated on the owner's word._

## What it is

The fifth patch of the alpha, on the same engine, the released Zeff 0.8.0 (commit
`6bfa6c55a5584e4e038fc828fef900a30f0856c3`, tag `v0.8.0`). Three things, each decided by the owner
on 24 November 2026: the app opens at the root of its address again and the landing page of
0.2.4 is removed (open decision #225); the loading screen's button is named **Start** (#225,
item 5); and the engine travels to a browser packed tight, about 2.9 MB in place of 4.1 MB
(#224). Its address is `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev`.

## What shipped

**The app at the root.** `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev` opens Zemp Lite itself, as it did before
0.2.4: a first visit meets the loading screen and then the project screen; a later visit, the
project screen. Projects and everything a browser kept are where they were: they belong to the
address, not to a path.

**The landing page removed.** The page that stood at the root in 0.2.4 (**Why Zemp Lite?**,
**See it work**, **Made for**, the counts and the links) is gone, with its tests; its text and
its table of the three regressions are shown nowhere. The page **What is counted** stays, and
leads back to Zemp Lite by one link.

**An address of 0.2.4 still works.** An address with `/app/` in it, of a project, of the naming
of a new one or of a page of Help, goes on to the same place at the root, and Back does not
return to it. An address of before 0.2.4, the root with a project after its `#`, is the app's own
again. The address for a page of Help is `/#/help` and a page after it.

**The button is Start.** The loading screen's button, **Open Zemp Lite →** in 0.2.4, says
**Start**, with no arrow and no name after it. It is grey until the engine runs and then opens,
and Return presses it, as before; the line under it is unchanged.

**The engine sent packed tight.** Until now the host packed the engine afresh each time it sent
it, at a low setting, so that about 4.1 MB travelled. The build now packs the engine once, at
brotli's highest setting, and the host sends that file as it is: about **2.9 MB** travels, 28 per
cent less. Nothing of the engine changes and no result changes: the browser unpacks the same
bytes, and the loading screen checks their digest as before. The loading screen's first step
gives the new size. A browser that does not take brotli is sent the engine as before.

**Each deployment tried beside the address first.** A build is now sent to a trial address of
the host, and every check of the address is made there in both engines; it reaches
`lite.zeff-lang.dev` only when the engine has started there. A build that could not start the
engine would never be put before a reader.

## Breaking changes

None for a project's files. On screen and in addresses:

- The app's address is the root; `/app/` goes on to it by itself. Nothing kept is lost.
- The landing page is gone. An address of one of its parts (the root with `#see-it-work` after
  it, say) opens the app.
- **Open Zemp Lite** is **Start**.

## Reference versions

As in 0.2.4: every output is held to what the released Zeff 0.8.0 command line prints, writes and
says for the same script, byte for byte. Gone with the landing page: the hold of its table to
what `zeff run` printed for the Scratch project's script; the nine sample projects are held as
before, the Scratch project's own run among the browser tests.

## What is left out, and why

The pages of a function and of a code reordered, and Home and search made aware of the reader's
work (open decision #223): its own release after this one, its order of parts still with the
owner. The other ways of making the download smaller that were measured on 24 November 2026 and
not decided: the table of internal names left out (about 0.1 MB more), an optimiser run over the
compiled program, compiling for size, and the help pages, examples and sample datasets as a
separate download (a change of the engine's design, for the engine's releases). **Open
folder…** as a working card: with the desktop app. The fonts of a figure (#222): an engine
change at Zeff 0.10.0.

## Known issues

- The known issues of 0.2.4 stand, among them: the reader test not run, the checks by hand not
  made, and the occasional wait of two to three seconds before ⌘↵ on GitHub's machines, its
  cause not found (seen at one of this release's deployments).
- The host's function that sends the packed engine is called once for each fetch of the engine
  by a browser that has not kept it, and the host's free plan allows 100,000 calls a day. A
  browser keeps the engine for a year, so only first visits and new versions call it; the limit
  is far off for an unannounced alpha, and is noted under risk R4.
- A window narrower than 1,200 pixels still says, at its top, that the app is laid out for 1,200
  pixels: true of a project's own screens, though the loading screen and the project screen have
  a phone's layout.
- In the routine's own tests, WebKit is sent the engine whole: it asks for brotli only over a
  secure connection, and the tests' server is a plain one. It meets the packed engine at the
  address, where the checks after each deployment hold it in both engines.
- A test browser does not keep a file of the engine's size between two visits, so "already in
  this browser" is tested by the browser's own record of a kept file, as at 0.2.4.

## Measures

_To be completed at the pre-release routine, over all the deployments of this release._ So far,
each measured at `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev` from GitHub's machines in the two engines:

- The engine as it travels from the host: **2.9 MB**, from the deployment that packed it:
  2,929,528 and 2,932,876 bytes as the two engines count them, where the two deployments before
  it sent 4,081,201 to 4,086,874 bytes. The host sends the build's packed file to a byte
  (2,929,528 bytes of 15,145,953 for GitHub's build of the engine; this machine's build packs to
  2,917,778 of 15,148,342). The measure of risk R4 reads 2,928.6 kB; the limit of R4: 5 MB.
- The first start of the app, timed to the engine running: before the packing, 1.3 s in
  Chromium and 1.6 and 2.0 s in WebKit; with the packed engine, 1.5 s and 2.0 s at the address
  and 0.8 s and 1.8 s at the trial address. The packing has not slowed the start.
- A run's figures first drawn: 379 to 604 ms after "done". A plot drawn afresh: 77 to 247 ms.
- ⌘↵ on one statement, from the key to "done": 30 to 92 ms; and in Chromium at one deployment
  2.8 s, all of it before the run began, which is the wait of the known issues.

## The validation suite

The routine on every change, run in full on GitHub in both browsers and on this Mac: format, lint,
types, the edition check, the Rust checks, the native tests of the worker, the worker's message
tests, the measure, the nine sample projects of Zeff 0.8.0 identical to what `zeff run` prints,
the ten tests of the engine's size, its packing and the host's function, 418 unit tests and 645
browser tests in Chromium and WebKit (3 skipped by design); at each deployment, the checks of
the address in the two engines, made at the trial address and then at the address itself: the
root is the app, an address of 0.2.4 goes on, a link into Help opens its page, the engine is
sent packed as the build packed it and whole to a browser that does not take brotli, and the
size the loading screen gives is the size sent.

_The record of the pre-release routine is added at step 6._

Not run or not checked at this release: the reader test, carried forward to Zeff 0.9.0 by the
owner's decision at 0.2.1, and the checks by hand (the keyboard alone on a real keyboard,
VoiceOver, Safari itself).

## To try it

Open `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev` in Chrome, Edge or Safari: the loading screen. Watch the engine
start (2.9 MB, once), press **Start**, and choose **Scratch project**. An address kept from
0.2.4, such as `https://lite.zeff-lang.dev/app/`, leads to the same place. The address is not
announced until the trademarks are filed (#37).
