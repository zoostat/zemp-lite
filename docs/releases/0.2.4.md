# Release 0.2.4 — The front door redrawn, and four changes of Help

The row of section 15 of the Zemp Specification: "0.2.4 | 0.8.0 | Zemp Lite's front door redrawn (#220): a landing page at the root of `lite.zeff-lang.dev` (the name large, **Open Zemp Lite** with the version beside it, bands for why, an example run on the carried engine, uses, the release's counts, and help and the source), its numbers and output taken from the carried engine at the build, and the app at `/app/` on the same origin; the loading screen with the three steps in one card under one bar, the size that travels, and the four promises in a band; the project screen without a heading, four cards in two rows (**New project…**, **Open project…**, which was **Open a project's zip…**; **Open folder…**, **Scratch project**), the five projects opened last in view with the lower edge fading, and the storage note in two columns; the name drawn as _Zemp_ with a raised _Lite_ badge wherever it is shown; and in Help (#220, items 6 to 9): hover in a script shows the usage block alone (F1 the whole page), **On this page** always shown as part of the page with no button or pane, each start of the app opening the Script place and the first click on Help showing its landing page with Topics closed, and Help's landing page in the look of these screens".

How to read this file: as the checklists of 0.1 to 0.2.3. Each step is small enough to finish and commit in one session. **[engine]** marks a hard or foundational piece (the address the app lives at, what the build takes from the engine, an address that opens a page of Help); **[function]** marks a screen, or words, over machinery that exists. The owner chooses the model for a session from the tag. Written on 24 November 2026, at "Start release 0.2.4"; no code is written before the owner's "go".

## What the reading found

1. **The previous release is tagged and the tree is clean:** `v0.2.3` on 8fd23e6; the commits after it change documents alone (the copy of the specification, the owner's rulings of the day). `docs/spec/` is brought to Zeff a110b4c, where the row of 0.2.4, #220 with its items 6 to 9 and the owner's "Yes, as drawn", and the change of #211's item 13 stand. Zeff 0.9.0 is not released, so the engine is Zeff 0.8.0, as in 0.2.3: the worker is not touched.
2. **The drawings**, all outside the repositories and approved by the owner: the landing page (`~/dev/handbook/zemp-lite-landing-mockup.html`); the loading and project screens in four states (`~/dev/handbook/zemp-lite-start-screens-mockup.html`, with `#loading`, `#ready`, `#first`, `#projects`); the four changes of Help (`~/dev/handbook/zemp-lite-0.2.4-help-mockup.html`); and section 9 of the design guide (`~/dev/handbook/zeff-design-guide.md`: "The name drawn", "Landing page", "Loading screen", "Project screen", with sizes and colours). Their builders are scratch files; the screens are built in the app's own components.
3. **The address.** Today the build is one page at the root, and a project's address is `/#/p/<project>`. The owner's projects and every reader's are kept in the browser's private file system, which belongs to the origin, not to a path: they are there at `/app/` as at the root. The engine is kept by the browser's own cache under the address of its file in `/assets/`, a name made from what the file holds; so long as the build keeps one `/assets/` for both pages, the engine of 0.8.0 keeps its address and is not fetched again by a browser that has it. An address kept from before (`/#/p/scratch`, `/#/new`) must still open the project: the root page forwards it to `/app/` with what follows the `#`. `counted.html` stays at the root and is linked from both pages.
4. **What the landing page takes from the engine at the build** (#220, item 1; #211, item 12): the engine's version; the counts of functions, packages and codes, which the worker's index of the help gives; the Scratch project's `main.zeff` and the print of its last statement, which the worker run under Node gives as the sample check runs it; the size that travels, which the measure of risk R4 computes. The build fails where one cannot be had. The loading screen shows the same travelling size (2.9 MB at 0.2.3) in place of the unpacked 15.1 MB.
5. **The landing page's links into the app** ("Getting started", "Finding help", "The Data place", "Exporting a project", "Open Help") need what the app does not have: an address that opens a page of Help. Help is a place inside a project, so such an address opens the Scratch project on the Help place at that page. It is a step of its own, before the landing page.
6. **The hover** is today the language server's own answer, which for a function's name is its whole help page; the index of the help, built at 0.2.3, already carries each function's usage and what it is called on, and the reading of a hover into the function in use (step 9g of 0.2.3) is there. The section 3 of the Zemp Specification says the hover gives "every function's help page": a correction for the coordinating session to make and log at the step.
7. **On this page** is a right pane of the frame of panes (#203), with its link in Help's bar, its command in ⌘K and its place in the tests of the panes; Topics' folded sections and the place shown are kept with the project's views, so the app can open on the place and the page it was left on. Items 7 and 8 of #220 change both.
8. **The name** "Zemp Lite" is written in 54 places of the screens' sources; "a project's zip" in 15. The pages of **Using Zemp Lite** name controls that this release renames or removes (**Open a project's zip…**, the link of **On this page**, the loading and project screens), and every picture shows a screen that changes: by the standing rule (#211, item 12) the pages are rewritten where they name them and every picture is made again.
9. **Found after 0.2.3, for this release:** one rule of `src/help/ListPage.svelte` asks for a font the app does not ship (#222); in Help's search, ↵ on an R or a Stata name opens the engine's sentence that no function has that name, and the name it suggests is not a link (question 3).
10. **Open decisions due at 0.2.4:** #220, decided, with its items 6 to 9. Risks the row touches: R4 (the landing page must not fetch the engine, and is measured; the download of the app is unchanged) and R10 (a visitor who does not see at once what Zemp Lite is does not open it).
11. **Carried from 0.2.3, not part of this row:** the reader test (owed at Zeff 0.9.0) and the checks by hand, the owner's; the pages of Help redrawn (#223), a release of its own after this one.

## Questions only the owner can answer

Each has a recommendation; "go, as recommended" takes them all.

1. **Where the app lives.** Taken as recommended in #220 and the owner's to overturn: the landing page at the root of the address and the app at `/app/`. _Recommended:_ so. A reader who returns to the root sees the landing page each time, one click from the app; an address kept from before with a project in it opens that project.
2. **Where "Report a problem" on the landing page leads.** _Recommended:_ to the page **Reporting a problem** of Using Zemp Lite, in the app, which says how a report is made and that nothing is sent. The other way: to the public list of issues of the repository, which invites reports from anyone before the address is announced (#37).
3. **The two faults of Help's search** (finding 9). _Recommended:_ mended in this release, since they are faults and not new design: ↵ opens the first match when the text typed is not a name, and a suggested name is a link. The other way: left for the release of #223.
4. **The pages of Using Zemp Lite.** _Recommended:_ as at 0.2.3, the paragraphs this release changes are put to the owner to read before the release (step 14), and changed on his word.
