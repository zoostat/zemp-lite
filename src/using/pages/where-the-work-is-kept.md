# Where the work is kept

Your projects are kept in this browser, on this computer, and nowhere else; export what matters.

## In this browser

A project lives in the private storage this browser gives the app. Nothing of it is sent anywhere: the engine runs in the browser, and so does everything else. Another browser, or another person's computer, does not have it.

What you type is saved as you type it. The line at the top of a project says `saved` beside the file's name when it is.

![The screen of projects: at the left the release and what the app is, and at the right the Scratch project, the ways to begin a project, and the sentence that says where projects are kept.](the-screen-of-projects)

## What can remove it

- Clearing the site's data in the browser removes every project.
- A browser may clear the storage by itself when the computer runs short of room. The app asks the browser not to, and the screen of projects says whether it has agreed.
- In a private window nothing is kept: a project lasts until the tab is closed, and the screen says so.

## The reminder

Where the browser may clear a project, or a project has not been exported for thirty days, the foot of the window says so, with **Export project…** beside it. A zip on this computer is the copy that is yours to keep: see [Exporting and opening a project](exporting-and-opening-a-project).
