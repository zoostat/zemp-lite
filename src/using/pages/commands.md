# Commands with ⌘K

Everything the window can do has a command: press ⌘K, type a word of it, and press Enter.

## The list of commands

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> on Windows and Linux), or the field **Search or run a command** at the top of the window. Type a word or two of what you want: the list narrows as you type, and the commands that hold your words as you typed them come first.

<kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>↑</kbd> choose a command, <kbd>↵</kbd> runs it, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the list. A command that has a key of its own shows it at the right of its row.

![The list of commands open over the script, before anything is typed: Run the script, Check the script, and under them the commands of the project and of the places.](the-commands)

## What is there

- To run: `Run the script` and `Check the script`; and, with the cursor in the script, `Run the statement at the cursor`, `Run section`, `Run to here` and `Run from here`.
- To move: `Place: Data` and the other places; `Open file:` with each file of the project; `Help: index of functions`.
- The panes: each is shown and hidden by its command, as `Script: show Project`.
- The project: `Project: new script…`, `Project: new folder…`, `Project: add files…` and `Project: export…`.
- The window: `Theme: dark`, `Code text: larger`, `Format the script`, `Output: clear`.
- `Report a problem`, when something has gone wrong.

## Why use it

A command is found by what it does, not by where its button is. Whatever a button of the window does, a command does too, so the whole app can be used from the keyboard.
