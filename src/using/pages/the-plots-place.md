# The Plots place

See every plot of the last run, compare them side by side, and save one at the size a journal or a slide asks for.

## The plots of a run

A plot is drawn under the statement that made it, in the script. Open Plots, the third of the icons down the left, to see the plots of the last run together: a strip of small pictures across the top, and the one you choose drawn large under it. In the script, the arrow beside a plot's note opens Plots on that plot.

![The Plots place: a strip of the run's plots across the top, with the sizes a plot is drawn at beside it; the chosen plot drawn large under it; and at the right a panel with its statement, the format and Save….](the-plots-place)

The panel at the right gives the plot's statement and its line, which goes to the statement in the script, and under them the format, **Save…** and the statement that saves the figure. **The plot**, at the end of the row, hides the panel and shows it again, so that the plot can have the place's whole width.

## Comparing

Tick **Compare** on two, three or four plots of the strip to see them side by side, each drawn to fit its share. **Unpin all** returns to one.

## The size, and saving

**Fit** draws the plot to fit the window. **One column**, **Two columns** and **Slide** show it at the size of a journal's column, a page's width and a slide, as it would be saved.

**Save…** saves the plot as SVG, PDF or PNG at the size shown, and gives the statement that saves the same file from a script. **Pop out** opens the plot in a window of its own, which redraws it when you change the window's size.
