# The Plots place

See every plot of the last run, compare them side by side, and save one at the size a journal or a slide asks for.

## The plots of a run

A plot is drawn under the statement that made it, in the script. Press **Plots** to see the plots of the last run together: a strip of small pictures at the left, and the one you choose drawn large.

![The Plots place: a strip of the run's plots at the left, the chosen plot drawn large in the middle, and at the right its statement and the size it would be saved at.](the-plots-place)

**Plots of the run** and **The plot**, in the bar, open and close the two panes. The pane at the right gives the plot's statement and its line, which goes to the statement in the script.

## Comparing

Tick **Compare** on two, three or four plots of the strip to see them side by side, each drawn to fit its share. **Unpin all** returns to one.

## The size, and saving

**Fit** draws the plot to fit the window. **One column**, **Two columns** and **Slide** show it at the size of a journal's column, a page's width and a slide, as it would be saved.

**Save…** saves the plot as SVG, PDF or PNG at the size shown, and gives the statement that saves the same file from a script. **Pop out** opens the plot in a window of its own, which redraws it when you change the window's size.
