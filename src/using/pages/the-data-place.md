# The Data place

Look at the frames a run made: sort them, keep some rows, search them, and read about each variable.

## The frames

Run the script, then open Data, the second of the icons down the left. The row over the table names the frame shown, with its size and the line of the statement that made it. Press the frame's name there for the list of the frames the run made, and press one to show it. In the script, the note of a frame opens Data on that frame.

![The Data place: the frames of the run listed at the left, and in the middle the rows of a frame as a table, each column headed by its name, its type and a small picture of its values.](the-data-place)

Each column is headed by the variable's name, its type, how many of its values are missing, and a small picture of how its values are spread. A missing value is written `NA`, never left blank.

## Sorting, keeping and searching

- Press the arrows in a column's head to sort the rows by it, and again to turn the order.
- **Keep rows where** takes a condition, as `year == 2012`, and keeps the rows it holds for, with a count of how many.
- **Search the cells** keeps the rows with a cell that holds what you type.

Nothing here changes the frame or the script: the view is yours alone. **Copy as Zeff** puts on the clipboard the statement that gives what you see, to paste into the script if you want to keep it.

## The variables

**Variables**, in the row, shows the variables of the frame in the table's place, and **Data** shows the table again. Untick a variable to hide it from the table; **Hide all** and **Show all** act on every one. Press a variable to read about it: its type, its rows and how many are missing, how its values are spread, and its statistics.
