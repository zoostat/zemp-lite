What is counted

Zemp Lite runs in your browser. Your projects, scripts and data are kept in this browser's private storage on your computer, and the engine that runs them runs here too: nothing of them is sent anywhere.

Counted

The host of this page, Cloudflare Pages, counts visits with its own cookie-free web analytics: how many visits there are, from which countries, with which browsers and on which kinds of device. These are counts of all visitors together. No cookie is set, nothing is kept in your browser for the count, and no visitor is told apart from another.

Never collected

  • your scripts, your data, your results or your plots;
  • your name, your email address, or an account: there is none to make;
  • a cookie, or anything else kept in your browser to know you again;
  • what you type or what you click.

Report a problem builds a bug report in your browser, which you read and save as a file of your own; it is never sent. You choose whether to attach it to an issue.
